Our Story

A kitchen built on hill-country memory.

Mezang means gathering — and that's what the table has always been for.

Tucked into 79 Tilla in West Chandmari, Mezang cooks the way Chakma households in the hills have for generations: bamboo shoot simmered slow, river fish fermented and finished with herbs, sticky rice steamed in leaf, and everything built to be shared, not portioned.

Alongside the traditional plates, the kitchen also turns out North Indian favourites — because a good table in Agartala welcomes everyone who sits at it. Come hungry; the format is all-you-can-eat, every single day.

Our kitchen

Rooted in Chakma tradition, cooked for Agartala.

Mezang brings Chakma home-style recipes to the table alongside familiar North Indian favourites, so a family can order one plate for grandparents and another for the kids without leaving anyone out.

The restaurant always runs on an all-you-can-eat format — food keeps coming until the table says stop, the way a proper home meal should work.

You'll find it 50 metres off Chakma Adam Road, tucked into West Chandmari's 79 Tilla neighbourhood — easy to reach, easy to settle into.
