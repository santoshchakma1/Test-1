Our kitchen

Rooted in Chakma tradition, cooked for Agartala.

Mezang brings Chakma home-style recipes to the table alongside familiar North Indian favourites, so a family can order one plate for grandparents and another for the kids without leaving anyone out.

The restaurant always runs on an all-you-can-eat format — food keeps coming until the table says stop, the way a proper home meal should work.

You'll find it 50 metres off Chakma Adam Road, tucked into West Chandmari's 79 Tilla neighbourhood — easy to reach, easy to settle into.
